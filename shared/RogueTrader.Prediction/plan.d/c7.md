## Implemented and focused coverage

`ShipPredictor` issues immutable PlayerInput records with contiguous ticks/sequences, a supplied queue bound and last accepted snapshot tick. Invalid controls, full capacity and exhausted identifiers preserve state. Reconciliation validates the controlled ship and acknowledgements before mutation, ignores stale/duplicate snapshots, expires completed ticks even when inputs were lost, and replays the pending suffix once. It retains the last issued sequence across expiration to prevent reuse.

The library predicts motion only using `ShipMotion`; hull/shield and gameplay effects remain authoritative. Baseline and static asteroid geometry are copied. A fresh matching context is required when the ship definition/sector changes.
