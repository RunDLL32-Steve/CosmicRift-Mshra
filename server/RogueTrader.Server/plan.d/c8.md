-   Queue C8 (branch `claude/project-thread-61bdln`, master [§13.5 anti-cheat principles](../../../ROGUE_TRADER_2_PLAN.md#135-anti-cheat-principles)): server-boundary validation added to `AuthoritativeSession`. Every threshold is NEW/provisional, pending approval; none comes from an original record. Rules: thrust or turn outside the documented [-1, 1] range of `ShipInput` is rejected (`OutOfRange`) instead of being clamped; an input acknowledging a snapshot tick later than the last simulated tick is rejected (`FutureSnapshot`); every submission, whatever its verdict, spends one unit of an allowance that refills by `InputsPerTick` (default 4: one input plus three redundant resends) per simulated tick up to `InputBurst` (default 120, two seconds at 60 Hz), and with none left it is `RateLimited`. All three are checked before anything is queued, so a rejected input changes no world state, queue or acknowledgement. A motion audit checks every tick run by `Step` or `Advance`: the controlled ship may move at most its definition's maximum speed times 1/60 s plus `DisplacementTolerance` (default 0.01 world units, covering float rounding and the 0.001 asteroid contact separation), and may not move between ticks except through the new `AuthoritativeSession.RestorePlayer`. Findings (`SpeedExceeded`, `MovedOutsideTick`) are counted and the latest 64 kept in `MotionViolations`; they are reported only, never enforced, because no ban or correction policy is approved. Clients send intent, not positions, so teleport detection guards the server's own state. Evidence: `tests/Unit/Server/InputValidationTests.cs` (24 cases: range ends and out-of-range values, future acknowledgements, allowance spending/refill/burst cap, a steady client resending every input three times never limited, rejected boundary inputs never changing the world digest, queue or acknowledgement across 120 ticks, full-thrust flight into an asteroid with no findings, a bypassing move flagged but not undone, a move between `Advance` calls, a speed finding from a restore onto an asteroid, the 64-finding cap, option validation). The 13 C6 tests pass unchanged. Not done: transport, per-connection identity, sequence-jump policing, any enforcement policy, original anti-cheat behavior. Status: in review, not merged by the author; no feature checkbox ticked.
