# M8 HUD, station screen and save/load — client view

Parent: [Client UI implementation plan](../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md). Queue item M8 in [TASK_QUEUE.md](../../../TASK_QUEUE.md) and [brief](../../../docs/tasks/m8.md). November 1, 2026.

-   **Boundary.** `RogueTrader.Trading.StationProfileSession` (Godot-free, unit-tested) owns the local `FileProfileRepository`, the `TransactionProcessor` and a `TradingService` that gates trades, repairs and upgrades on docking. The client only renders `session.Profile` and raises requests. Every transaction is saved before it is acknowledged.
-   **Station data.** The client docks at the [derived Trading Outpost](../../../data/stations/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md): original recipes, repairs and the 35 original upgrade/armor rows at their original prices; placement, wallet, capacity, repair price and effects are NEW.
-   **HUD** (`FlightHud`): credits, cargo, held items and owned upgrades from the saved profile; a dock hint inside the radius; profile status, save folder and **Reload saved profile** in the pause menu.
-   **Station screen** (`StationScreen`, key **F**, Escape undocks): Trading Outpost (trade 1 / trade all per recipe), Repairs (disabled and refused without charge at full hull or when destroyed) and Upgrades (grouped by family, effect summary, BUY / ACTIVE / OWNED). The world does not tick while docked; refusals are explained and change nothing.
-   **Ship condition.** Hull, position and heading are saved to the profile (schema 3, see the [persistence entry](../../../server/RogueTrader.Persistence/plan.d/m8.md)) on pause, dock, undock, repair, reset, quit and every ten seconds of flight, and restored on launch. A ship saved while destroyed relaunches at spawn.
-   **Upgrades.** `UpgradeEffects.Apply` builds the flying ship from the owned upgrades; buying one rebuilds the world keeping position, heading and hull.
-   **Pickups.** Collected cargo is committed as one `GrantReward`; if that is refused the units stay in the hold, the profile is reloaded when the save itself failed, and the commit is retried once a second.
-   **Verification.** `--smoke-test` runs `ClientStationSmokeTest` against an isolated save folder: HUD wallet, pickup kept during a suppressed save and then committed, F docking, recipe trade, repair refusal and repair, upgrade effect, unaffordable refusal, ship save on pause, reload and full reopen. `--capture-station --station-tab=N` captures a station tab.
-   **Not done.** Native HUD/store art and flow (RT-F025/F026), every undecoded original rule listed in the station plan, HyperDrive abilities, upgrade resale. M9 owns the exit/relaunch acceptance test. No original feature box or count changes.
