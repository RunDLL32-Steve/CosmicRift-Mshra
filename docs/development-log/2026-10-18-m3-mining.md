## Implemented generic slice

- Optional strict external `MiningCatalog`: finite positive-quantity deposits associated with existing asteroids, and one optional installed mining capability. Missing mining data/capability leaves extraction disabled. Definitions snapshot caller lists and reject null/empty/duplicate/unknown references, invalid quantities/equipment and nonfinite/overflowing combined geometry.
- `ShipInput.Mine` defaults false, preserving existing three-argument callers. The pure 60 Hz authority moves and collects existing swept pickups, extracts at most one cycle if Mine is held and ready, then collects new drops only at the current ship position. Energy/fire/projectile processing follows. Destroyed ships cannot mine.
- NEW/provisional policy: first compatible reachable nonempty asteroid in sector catalog order; reach is ship radius + asteroid radius + positive equipment range, inclusive at the boundary. Range zero is rejected because the collision solver separates contact by 0.001 world units. Failed attempts do not consume a cycle; a successful extraction is immediately ready on its first intent tick and again after CycleTicks. No original cadence is inferred.
- NEW/provisional placement: a pile appears just outside the facing asteroid surface (asteroid radius + configured pile radius toward the ship; +X fallback at coincident centers). Generated ids monotonically skip configured pile-id collisions. Finite deposit units move to a pile, then through existing CargoHold pickup; full/partial/int-limited holds leave the remainder in space. No currency is awarded, no items regenerate, and no quantities become zero/negative piles.
- Replay format **v2** stores the Mine byte and includes deposit item/quantity, next-mining tick, generated-pile counter and pile position/radius in the world digest. **v1 is explicitly rejected** because its inputs and state hashes have different semantics. Replays require the same initial catalog/cargo/mining configuration via the world factory. The separate Protocol codec does not yet carry Mine.
