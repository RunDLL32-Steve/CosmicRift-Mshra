## Evidence rules

| Classification | Use |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED | The specific claim is present in an original source that was actually read, or in sufficiently direct surviving evidence. Scope the claim to the documented game/version/mode. |
| COMMUNITY | Firsthand recollection, read in the linked discussion, with no independent original confirmation of that detail. |
| RECONSTRUCTED | An estimate from footage or screenshots actually inspected, with a locator and method. The limited observed video frame qualifies; no native physics measurement is claimed. |
| NEW | A Rogue Trader 2.0 choice, including every current simulation constant, balancing value and proposed formula. |
| UNKNOWN | Exact original behavior is missing, including secondary claims whose cited primary evidence does not establish that detail. |

Confidence applies to the historical claim, not to implementation correctness. High means an explicit original statement; medium means a detailed recollection or useful secondary summary; low means ambiguity or no direct evidence. An accessed page is not blanket verification of every claim associated with it. A generic Cosmic Rift weapon catalog does not establish Rogue Trader equipment availability.

The master plan makes mining, earning rewards through combat, buying improvements, persistence and multiplayer mandatory recreation targets. The user-designated canonical archive now supplies original content/stat records; read and preserve those definitions before inventing replacements. Remaining uncertainty concerns their engine interpretation or historical statements outside the inspected data. The existing configurable NEW prototype remains separately labeled and does not replace the reproduction target.
